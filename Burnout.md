# What causes burnout?
1. No boundaries- software engineers can do their job any time, any where, especially with WFH. 
- Fix: Create an arbitrary deadline to hit every single day. For example, at 5:30pm go play tennis or have dinner with someone. \
This gives you something to look forward to and will force you to leave your computer and do something else

2. There is no limit to how deep you can go with the code
- Fix:  Just trust the API \
      Don't aim for "mastery" \
      Share your vulnerability
  
3. Unclear roles
- Fix:  Create structure about what work you have to do on the team

4. Unclear communication
- Fix:  Overcommunicate to avoid surprises

## How to avoid Burnout
*from the Primeagen*

1. Don't place your worth in your work. \
Have other things in life that give you a purpose

2. Give grace. \
You dont realise how much weight people have on their shoulders

3. Investigate if you are just bored. \
Maybe you are not being challanged enough.
